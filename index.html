Featured product · EdTech

College2Company

A mobile-first career platform that helps students and recent graduates go from campus to their first job, and helps employers find early-career talent.

Launch your career with confidence

College2Company brings company research, job search, networking and recruiting into one app. Students explore employers through real reviews and salary insights, find internships and entry-level roles near them, and connect with alumni and recruiters.

For students & graduates

  • Resume builder that highlights skills and experience
  • Job matching with location filters for internships, graduate programs and entry-level roles
  • Application tracking, with automatic applications to roles you qualify for
  • Interview prep: company profiles, insights and employee reviews

For employers & recruiters

  • Employer dashboard to post jobs and manage candidates
  • Talent search across candidate profiles and resumes
  • Direct messaging with students, graduates and alumni
